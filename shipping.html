Shipping and Delivery

For local and international customers

Shipping fees for both local and international orders are the responsibility of the customer and will be calculated at checkout.

For international orders, any applicable taxes, customs duties, import duties, and other fees are the responsibility of the customer and must be paid in order for the parcel to clear customs.

Free Local Shipping (Malaysia)

Free local shipping is available on orders of RM 100 or more.

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful and your parcel is returned to us, you will be notified via email. Please note that Le Trèfle is not responsible for failed deliveries due to an incorrect or incomplete shipping address provided by the customer. Any additional shipping costs required for redelivery will be the responsibility of the customer.
