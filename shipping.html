Shipping and Delivery

Our delivery policy

Hey you! It’s important to us that your order reaches you, and we’ll do our best to ensure that it happens in the most efficient way. Please make sure that your preferred shipping address and contact details are clearly stated and completed at the check-out page to allow for a smooth delivery process from our end!

For Local Customers (Malaysia)

Free local shipping is available on orders of RM 100 or more. For orders below RM 100, shipping fees will be calculated at checkout. All local deliveries are dispatched within 3-5 working days from the day you receive your shipment email.

Should the delivery fail and your package be returned to us, you would receive another email notifying you. Please note that Le Trèfle would not be responsible in the event where a wrong or incomplete address has been provided by the customer.

For International Customers

Shipping fees for international orders are the responsibility of the customer and will be calculated at checkout based on location.

All taxes, customs and import duties and fees are the responsibility of the customer, and must be paid by you in order for your parcel to clear customs. Fees will be shown upon checkout when address has been given.
