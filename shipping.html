Shipping and Delivery

Shipping Policy

Hey! We are committed to delivering your order safely and efficiently. Please ensure that your shipping address and contact information are accurate and complete when placing your order ♥

For Local Customers (Malaysia)

Shipping fee will be charge for local orders at checkout. Free local shipping is available on orders of RM 100 or more.

Should the delivery fail and your package be returned to us, you would receive another email notifying you.

Please note that Le Trèfle would not be responsible in the event where a wrong or incomplete address has been provided by the customer.

For International Customers

Shipping charges for international orders are calculated at checkout based on the destination.

Any taxes, customs duties, import duties, brokerage fees, or other charges imposed by the destination country are the responsibility of the customer.

Please note that Le Trèfle would not be responsible for any delays or additional charges resulting from customs clearance.
